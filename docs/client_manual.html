Chapter 5

Checkout: 1-Tap GPS Finder & Payment Methods

Designed for Egyptian mobile shoppers: zero manual typing of long governorates and districts.

📍 1-Tap GPS Address Finder

Clients simply tap [ 📍 Use My Current Location ]. The system auto-populates:

1. Governorate
Matches all 27 Governorates including Sahel, Matrouh, Cairo, Alex.
2. City / District
Extracts compound, village, or neighborhood (e.g. Stella Heights, Tagamoa).
3. Street Address
Extracts road name and main area landmarks.

⚠️ Building / Villa & Apartment Number is mandatory (*) and left empty for courier delivery accuracy!

Payment Method How It Works Client Experience
Cash on Delivery (COD) Pay upon delivery directly to courier across all 27 governorates. Zero upfront payment; client can inspect package before handing cash.
Instapay Transfer Instant bank/wallet transfer to official number 01204000195 (armia emad). Client uploads transfer screenshot directly on page; atelier verifies instantly.

⚡ Pre-Purchase Checkout Upselling ("Complete Your Look")
